Sound set harness

Every clip in the library, decoded and measured. rms is raw loudness as generated; norm is the correction sfx.js applies to bring a take in line with its siblings; mixed is what actually reaches the bus. A take flagged dud is far quieter than its siblings and should be regenerated rather than amplified. onset is dead air before the sound starts — skipped at playback, but a large one means the clip was generated badly.
